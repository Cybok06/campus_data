Phone Numbers Page Guide
  • Unique Phone Numbers shows all unique phone numbers that have placed orders in the system.
  • New Phone Numbers shows numbers whose first-ever order happened in the selected period. Use All time or a Custom date range.
  • Export lets you download the current result as Excel or PDF. In the unique view, you can export all, a specific service, or a specific network.
  • Block New Numbers controls whether brand new numbers are allowed to order. When it is ON, only numbers that already have order history can place orders.
  • When a number is blocked, customer dashboard and store page will stop it before carting and show a message telling the user to contact Admin.
  • The summary cards show counts for the current view, including the count of new numbers in the new-numbers tab.
  • Pagination is set to 20 records per page for faster loading.
